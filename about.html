About Milan Panek

Milan Panek’s passion for health and fitness began in his teenage years and has since grown into a lifelong vocation. He is a natural professional bodybuilding champion and has competed across Europe and the UK, bringing home multiple podium finishes. Off the stage, Milan’s greatest joy comes from helping others realise their potential.

With over two decades of coaching experience, Milan has guided clients of all ages and abilities through fat‑loss transformations, muscle building, strength gains, competition prep, postural correction and rehabilitation. He holds recognised qualifications in personal training, nutrition and sports massage, ensuring that every programme is holistic and science‑based.

What truly sets Milan apart is his empathy and commitment. He understands the mental and emotional challenges that accompany physical change. By combining a results‑driven approach with motivation and humour, he fosters a supportive environment in which every client feels empowered.

Outside of the gym, Milan enjoys cooking nutritious meals, expanding his knowledge of physiology and spending time with his family. If you’re looking for a coach who lives and breathes what he teaches, you’ve found him.
