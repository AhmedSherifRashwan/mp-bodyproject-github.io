Our Approach to Training

At MP‑BodyProject, we believe in tailoring every aspect of your programme to suit your individual goals and circumstances. Below you’ll find an overview of the core pillars that underpin our coaching.

One‑on‑One Coaching

Every client starts with a detailed consultation to determine your goals, experience, lifestyle and preferences. Based on this information Milan designs a bespoke training plan, guiding you through each session with meticulous attention to form and intensity. One‑on‑one coaching ensures your workouts evolve as you progress and keeps you accountable.

You’ll receive regular assessments, program adjustments and continuous motivation. Milan’s supportive, no‑nonsense style encourages you to push beyond perceived limits while still having fun.

Diet & Nutrition

Training is only half the equation – nutrition plays an equally vital role. Milan works closely with you to create realistic and sustainable meal plans that support your objectives, whether fat loss, muscle gain or enhanced performance.

Expect guidance on macronutrient breakdown, meal timing, portion sizes and supplement use where appropriate. His recommendations are grounded in current science and tailored to your tastes and lifestyle.

Training Tips & Education

Milan goes beyond simply telling you what to do. He educates you on why each exercise, rep scheme and rest period matters. Understanding the principles behind your programme equips you to train smarter now and in the future.

He’ll also provide mobility drills, warm‑up routines, recovery techniques and lifestyle advice to optimise your progress. Knowledge is power – and Milan shares it generously.
