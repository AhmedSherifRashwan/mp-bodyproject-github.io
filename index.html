About Milan Panek

Milan Panek is a seasoned professional with more than 20 years of experience in natural bodybuilding, physique coaching and performance nutrition. As a certified personal trainer and natural pro athlete, he’s helped dozens of clients—from complete beginners to competition‑ready athletes—transform their bodies and mindsets.

His philosophy is simple: work hard, work smart and enjoy the process. Every programme is personalised and backed by solid nutritional guidance. Whether you’re aiming to lose fat, build lean muscle, compete on stage or simply adopt a healthier lifestyle, Milan will be there every step of the way to motivate and educate you.
